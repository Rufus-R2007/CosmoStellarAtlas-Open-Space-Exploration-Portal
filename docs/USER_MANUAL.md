## 1. Welcome to CosmoStellarAtlas

**CosmoStellarAtlas** is an interactive, cinematic digital space exploration platform engineered as a futuristic mission-control console and planetary observatory. It provides an intuitive interface for surveying planets in 3D, inspecting Mars rover operations, replaying historical missions, simulating asteroid planetary defense encounters, querying local space intelligence, and tracking educational achievements.

## 2. Global Navigation & Hotkeys

- **Sidebar Navigation:** Use the left navigation drawer to move between the 9 core modules:
  1. **Observatory Dashboard (`#/`)**
  2. **Planet Explorer (`#/planets`)**
  3. **Cosmic Time Machine (`#/timeline`)**
  4. **Mission Control (`#/missions`)**
  5. **Mars Rover Explorer (`#/rovers`)**
  6. **Asteroid Watch & Lab (`#/asteroids`)**
  7. **Space Intelligence (`#/intelligence`)**
  8. **Learning Hub (`#/learn`)**
  9. **Personal Vault (`#/vault`)**
- **Command Palette (`Ctrl + K` or `Cmd + K`):** Instantly search across all cataloged planets, missions, rovers, and asteroids from anywhere in the application. Use `↑` and `↓` arrow keys to navigate and `Enter` to jump directly to the target record.
- **Offline Provenance Badge:** Located in the top header navbar. Click this button at any time to open a transparent modal disclosing data sources, reference dates, and synthetic demonstration boundaries.
- **System Settings:** Click the gear icon in the header to adjust Visual FX (`High`, `Minimal`, `Off`) to accommodate low-power hardware or reduced-motion preferences.

## 3. Module Operations Walkthrough

### 3.1 Observatory Dashboard (`#/`)
- **Celestial Earth Centerpiece:** Click and drag the glowing 3D Earth globe to inspect atmospheric day/night terminators and orbiting telemetry satellites.
- **Dynamic Fleet Statistics:** Review high-level metrics dynamically aggregated from bundled records.
- **Cosmic Time Machine Preview:** Scrub the year slider to view historical spaceflight milestones for any landmark year.
- **Mission Activity Overview:** Review the interactive Recharts mission launch distribution across decades and destination classes.

### 3.2 Planet Explorer & 3D Planetarium (`#/planets`)
- **Interactive 3D Viewport:**
  - Click and drag anywhere on the planetary globe to rotate.
  - Scroll with your mouse wheel or use the `+` / `-` buttons to zoom in and out.
  - Click the **Reset View** button to restore standard orientation.
  - Click **Expand Viewport** for a full-screen planetarium experience.
- **2D Accessible Fallback:** If your device does not support WebGL, or if WebGL is disabled, CosmoStellarAtlas automatically switches to a high-speed banded 2D HTML5 canvas renderer without interruption.
- **Solar System Navigator:** Use the bottom carousel to browse worlds, or switch to **Schematic Mode** to observe true heliocentric orbital ordering from Sol.
- **Relative Weight Simulator:** Enter your Earth weight (in kilograms) in the telemetry panel to immediately calculate your exact physical weight on the selected world ($W = W_{\text{Earth}} \times g_{\text{rel}}$).
- **Planetary Comparison Lab:** Click **Compare World** to place any two planets side-by-side. Inspect proportional visual scale discs and review automated comparative insights generated by the deterministic comparison engine.

### 3.3 Cosmic Time Machine (`#/timeline`)
- **Temporal Chronology Scrubber:** Drag the continuous slider spanning **1957 to 2026+**.
- **Decade Quick-Jump Buttons:** Click pills (`1950s`, `1960s`, ..., `2020s`) to immediately advance time.
- **Epoch Overview Panel:** Review active operating spacecraft and contemporaneous historical milestones for your selected year.
- **Cosmic Time Capsules:** Browse curated breakthrough achievements categorized into Firsts, Lunar, Deep Space, Planetary, and Space Telescopes.

### 3.4 Mission Control & Interactive Replay (`#/missions`)
- **Mission Directory Archive:** Browse 20 curated missions across NASA, ESA, JAXA, Roscosmos, ISRO, and CNSA.
- **Multi-Attribute Filters:** Filter missions by agency, target celestial body, architecture (Orbiter, Lander, Rover, Flyby), or launch decade.
- **Mission Detail Dossier:** Click **Inspect Dossier** on any mission card to view engineering specifications (dry mass, launch vehicle, scientific payload array) and read the 5-chapter educational narrative.
- **Interactive Mission Replay Engine:** Click **Launch Mission Replay** on eligible missions (e.g. Apollo 11). Use the playback controls (**Play**, **Pause**, **0.5x / 1x / 2x speed**, **Milestone Scrubber**) to experience data-driven 2D HTML5 canvas animations tailored to distinct milestone flight scenes (launchpad, orbital insertion, deep space cruise, surface descent, scientific survey).

### 3.5 Mars Rover Explorer & Photo Discovery Lab (`#/rovers`)
- **Fleet Selection:** Switch between all 5 NASA Mars explorers: **Perseverance**, **Curiosity**, **Opportunity**, **Spirit**, and **Sojourner**.
- **Engineering Specifications:** Inspect mobility suspension, MMRTG nuclear power generators, and expand the onboard science instruments accordion.
- **Martian Sol Scrubber:** Drag the Sol slider to filter surface photos captured on specific Martian solar days.
- **Interactive Image Inspection Lab:** Click any rover photograph to launch the high-precision modal:
  - Drag to pan across Martian bedrock strata.
  - Zoom in up to $4.0\times$ magnification.
  - Adjust Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation sliders to uncover shadowed formations or balance bright dust dunes.
  - Review normalized percentage bounding box annotations highlighting scientific instruments and geological features.
  - Write and save personal field research observations attached to that image.
- **Topographic Surface Journey:** Follow the sequential waypoint traverse map tracing historic landing sites across Jezero Delta, Mount Sharp, and Meridiani Planum.

### 3.6 Asteroid Watch & Planetary Defense What-If Lab (`#/asteroids`)
- **2D Orbital Encounter Explorer:** Select any Near-Earth Object from the target dropdown. Watch its animated hyperbolic trajectory sweep past Earth and Lunar orbit ($1\text{ LD} = 384,400\text{ km}$) with a live distance HUD and closest approach perigee vector.
- **Chronological Close-Approach Scrubber:** Browse upcoming encounters (2026–2035) and click **Test in What-If Lab** to pre-populate simulation parameters.
- **Flagship Planetary Defense What-If Lab:**
  - Adjust the **Diameter Slider** ($10\text{--}1000\text{ m}$) to observe cubic energy scaling: doubling diameter ($100\text{m} \rightarrow 200\text{m}$) multiplies kinetic energy by $8\times$.
  - Adjust the **Velocity Slider** ($5\text{--}40\text{ km/s}$) to observe quadratic energy scaling: doubling velocity ($10 \rightarrow 20\text{ km/s}$) multiplies kinetic energy by $4\times$.
  - Adjust the **Illustrative Miss Distance Slider**: Observe that miss distance is purely visual and strictly never alters kinetic energy calculations or fabricates collision probabilities.
  - Test **Scenario Presets**: Click Chelyabinsk-Scale (~20m airburst), Tunguska-Scale (~60m regional blast), or DART/Dimorphos-Scale (~160m cratering impact).

### 3.7 Space Intelligence & Smart Discovery (`#/intelligence`)
- **Ask the Atlas Assistant:** Ask natural language questions in plain English (e.g. *"What is Mars?"*, *"Compare Earth and Jupiter"*, *"Tell me about Apollo 11"*, *"Which planet has the most moons?"*). The system parses your intent using deterministic rules and displays markdown factsheets with verified citations in $<3\text{ ms}$ without external AI APIs.
- **Universal Smart Search:** Type any keyword (e.g. *"Jezero"*, *"Apophis"*, *"Titan"*) to query the weighted inverted catalog index.
- **Cosmic Discovery Knowledge Graph:** Explore the interactive 2D directed relational network (SVG). Click any node to highlight connections, toggle the **1-Hop Neighborhood Focus**, or switch to the accessible semantic list view.
- **Explainable Recommendations:** Review personalized exploration recommendations with human-readable rationales derived from your active session telemetry.
- **Algorithmic Evidence Audit Panel:** Click **Audit Rule Logic** to review the exact mathematical derivation and database records examined for any recommendation.

### 3.8 Interactive Learning Hub (`#/learn`)
- **Curriculum Trails:** Choose from 4 astrophysics trails:
  1. *Journey Through the Solar System* (`SOL-CURR-01`)
  2. *Mission to Mars* (`MARS-CURR-02`)
  3. *Guardians of Earth* (`NEO-CURR-03`)
  4. *Chronicles of Space Exploration* (`HIST-CURR-04`)
- **Interactive Lessons:** Click **Start Lesson** to read comprehensive modules featuring learning objectives, callout boxes (`fact`, `warning`, `tip`), key takeaways, and one-click shortcuts to connected simulation labs. Click **Mark as Completed** when finished.
- **Certification Quizzes:** Click **Take Certification Quiz** to test your knowledge with 6 multiple-choice questions. Score $\ge 70\%$ to earn official certification badges and unlock achievements in your Passport.

### 3.9 Personal Space Vault & Explorer Passport (`#/vault`)
- **Profile HUD:** Review your explorer callsign, clearance level (Levels 1–5), rank insignia, storage quota footprint, and client-side data sovereignty disclosure.
- **Universal Bookmarks:** Filter saved discoveries across All, Planets, Missions, Rovers, Images, and Asteroids.
- **Collections:** Create custom research shelves (e.g. *"Outer Planets Study"*), assign bookmarks to shelves, and organize field research.
- **Exploration Field Journal:** Create, edit, and search field notes with tags (e.g. `#astrophysics`, `#mars`).
- **Explorer Passport:** Review your holographic credential card showing verified counts of surveyed planets, completed simulations, and certified quizzes.
- **Interactive Constellation Progress Map:** Explore the interactive $800 \times 480$ SVG starfield map. Click achievement stars to inspect requirements, observe dual-ignition illuminated vector edges as milestones unlock, or toggle the accessible list view.
- **Personal Analytics:** View interactive Recharts graphs tracking exploration activity distribution, quiz scores, and curriculum completion.
- **Data Backup & Recovery:**
  - Click **Export Vault Data (JSON)** to download your local data snapshot.
  - Upload a previously saved backup file using **Merge** or **Replace** mode.
  - Click **Reset Vault** (with double confirmation) to safely return to default state.
