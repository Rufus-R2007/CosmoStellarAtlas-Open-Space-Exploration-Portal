Mission Significance & Astrodynamic Leap

At 20:17:40 UTC on July 20, 1969, the Lunar Module Eagle touched down in the southwestern plains of Mare Tranquillitatis. Neil Armstrong's manual pitch override navigated clear of a boulder-strewn crater field with less than 25 seconds of descent propellant remaining.

verified Why This Event Matters

Apollo 11 validated trans-lunar injection vectors, active radar altimetry descent guidance, and humanity's capacity to escape Earth's gravitational well, retrieve extraterrestrial planetary samples, and execute rendezvous maneuvers in orbital lunar space.
